# शराप (SHRAP)
### Dhriv ki Kahani, Chandraprish ke shabdon me

---

## Adhyay 1 — Kripal Ghati ka Rahasya

Kahani shuru hoti hai us gaon se jahan pahaad apni chhaya sham dhalte hi poore ghar-aangan par bichha diya karte the. Yah ek chhota sa gaon tha, jiska naam itihaas ki kisi kitaab me darj nahi tha. Parantu iski galiyon me utni hi kahaniyan basi thi jitni kisi bade nagar ki haveliyon me. 

Us gaon ke sabse sammanit ghar me ek purush rehta tha jiska naam tha Kripaldev. Kripaldev lambe kad ke, gehri aankhon wale, aur aisi aawaz ke maalik the jo jab bhi bolti thi, to poora aangan chup ho jaata tha. Unki yah pratishtha unke pita ne unhe virasat me di thi, aur unhone use apni mehnat se aur bhi ooncha kar diya tha. Gaon ke log unhe apna margdarshak maante the. Chhoti se chhoti samasya ho ya bada faisla, log sidhe Kripaldev ke dwar par aate the. Aur Kripaldev ne bhi kabhi kisi ko nirash, haath khali laut kar nahi jaane diya.

Kripaldev ka vivah Krupiri se hua tha. Wah ek saral, sundar aur atyant sanskari stree thi. Parantu is vivah ki neev prem par nahi, balki do parivaron ke samajik samjhaute par rakhi gayi thi. Kripaldev jab pandrah varsh ke the tabhi unke pita ne yah rishta tay kar diya tha, bina yah soche ki unke bete ka dil kahin aur bhi bhatak sakta hai. 

Aur wahi hua bhi. Kripaldev jab unnees varsh ke hue aur vyapar ke silsile me pahli baar us door ke gaon gaye jise log "Sarvya Ghati" kaha karte the, wahan unki mulakat ek yuvati se hui. Jiska naam tha Raadhna. Raadhna kisi sadharan gharane ki nahi thi. Wah ek saadhvi thi jo us parvat ki chhaya me rehti thi jise log "Kripal Ghati Parvat" kaha karte the. Kaha jata hai ki us parvat ka naam khud Raadhna ne hi rakha tha, Kripaldev ke prem me. Aisa prem jo samay ke sath itna gehra ho gaya ki us stree ne poore ek parvat ka naam apne priyatam ke naam par rakh diya. Yah baat sunne me jitni romanchak lagti hai, utni hi dukhad bhi thi, kyuki yah prem kisi saamajik maryada ke daayre me nahi tha.

Kripaldev aur Raadhna ki pahli mulakat ek mele me hui thi, jahan Raadhna apne aashram ke liye bhiksha maangne aayi thi. Kripaldev ne jaise hi use dekha, unke hriday me kuch aisa mahsoos hua jo unhone apni poori zindagi me kabhi nahi kiya tha. Unki baaten shuru hui. Phir mulakaten badhi, aur dheere dheere yah mulakaten ek gahre prem me badal gayi. 

Kripaldev jaante the ki unka vivah ho chuka hai. Wah jaante the ki unke ghar me ek stree unka intezaar karti hai. Parantu prem, jaisa ki kaha jata hai, tark ki seema me nahi bandha. Kripaldev bhi is prem ke aage vivash ho gaye. Wah baar-baar apne vyapar ke bahane us ghati me jaate rahe. Kabhi ek hafte ke liye, kabhi mahine bhar ke liye. Aur har baar Krupiri ghar par akele unki raah dekhti. Usne chupchaap akele haathon se ghar ki, khet ki, aur badhte hue putra Dhriv ki dekhbhal ka zimma utha liya.

Dhriv, jo is kahani ka naayak hai, Kripaldev aur Krupiri ka ekmatra putra tha. Uska janm gaon ke sabse shubh mahine me hua tha. Uske janm ke samay poore gaon me utsav ka mahaul chhaya tha. Kripaldev ne apne navjaat putra ko hathon me uthate hue garv se kaha tha, "Yah balak ek din is poori ghati ka naam roshan karega!" 

Dhriv apne pita ko devta samaan maanta tha. Jab bhi Kripaldev ghar par hote, wah Dhriv ko apni godi me baithakar veer purushon ki kahaniyan sunaya karte. Wah use tir chalana sikhate. Use ghode par baithna sikhate. Dhriv ke liye Kripaldev sirf pita nahi the; wah uski sabse badi prerna the, uski poori duniya the. Aur shayad yahi wajah thi ki jab sach saamne aaya, to us masoom balak ke andar kuch aisa toota jo kabhi poori tarah se jud nahi paya.

Sach saamne aane ka madhyam bane ek Brahmin, jo us gaon ke mandir me pooja karaya karte the. Unhone ek din vyapar ke silsile me Sarvya Ghati ki taraf yatra ki. Wahan unhone apni ankhon se Kripaldev ko Raadhna ke sath sandehaspad halat me madira paan karte dekha. Yah drishya dekhkar Brahmin ka man vichlit ho gaya. Wah janate the ki gaon me Krupiri aur Dhriv unki raah dekh rahe hai. Unhone socha ki yah baat chhupayein ya bataye? Parantu unke antah-karan ki awaaz ne unhe majboor kar diya. 

Jab Brahmin wapas laute, to unhone sidhe Krupiri ke dwar par dastak di. Unhone bade sanjidgi se, bina koi ati rang bhare, theek wahi bata diya jo unhone dekha tha.

Krupiri apne pati par apni saans se bhi zyada vishwas karti thi. Brahmin ki baat sunte hi uske haath se chaaval ki thali chhoot gayi. Zameen par anaj bikhar gaya. 

"Nahi Pandit ji, aap galatfehmi me hai," Krupiri ne apne kaanpte hue haathon ko chhupate hue kaha. "Mere swami aise nahi ho sakte."

Parantu Brahmin ne bade vinamra, par drdh swar me apni baat dohrayi. "Krupiri beti, mai apni ankhon dekhi baat kah raha hoon." 

Yah sunte hi Krupiri ke andar ek bhayanak yudh shuru ho gaya. Ek taraf uska vishwas tha, jo varshon ke saath se bana tha; doosri taraf ek anjaana, thanda dar jo dheere-dheere uski atma me utarta ja raha tha.

Krupiri ne faisla kiya ki jab Kripaldev ghar lautenge, tab wah unse seedha sawal karegi. Parantu use dar tha ki agar usne yah sawal seedha kiya, to Kripaldev ko bura lag sakta hai. Isliye usne ek madhyam nikala. Jab Kripaldev vyapar se wapas laute, to Krupiri ne aangan me apne chhah baras ke putra Dhriv ko Kripaldev ki god me baithaya.

Usne dhimi lekin thehri hui aawaz me poochha, "Swami, mujhe apne putra Dhriv ki kasam khakar batayein. Kya Brahmin ki baaton me koi sachhai hai?" 

Yah ek atyant kathin pal tha. Kisi pita ke liye apne putra ki kasam se badhkar kuch nahi hota. Krupiri ne yahi soch kar yah tarika chuna tha ki agar Kripaldev jhoot bolna bhi chahenge, to apne masoom bete ki kasam ke aage toot jayenge.

Kripaldev, jo apni jabardasti ki shaadi me bhale hi uljhe hue the, par Dhriv se unka prem sacha aur poora tha. Dhriv ki jhooti kasam khane ka sawal hi paida nahi hota tha. 

Kripaldev ki nazrein jhuk gayin. Unhone bhari man se apna jurm qabool kar liya. "Haan Krupiri, yah sach hai. Mera sambandh Raadhna se hai, aur yah kayi varshon se chal raha hai." Unhone yahan tak keh diya ki Krupiri se unka vivah unki apni ichha se nahi, balki parivaar ki majboori se hua tha. Unka sacha prem keval Raadhna ke sath tha.

Yah sunte hi Krupiri ke pairon tale zameen khisak gayi. Aath varsh ka saath, aath varshon ka atoot vishwas... ek hi jhatke me kaanch ki tarah toot kar bikhar gaya.

Yah bhi ek vichitra baat thi ki Kripaldev ne apni poori zindagi me kabhi ek din bhi aisa ehsaas nahi hone diya ki unhone apne pariwar ki zimmedari se munh moda ho. Ghar ka har kaam, Dhriv ki har zaroorat, aur Krupiri ka poora samman—Kripaldev ne sab kuch bina kisi kami ke poora kiya tha. 

Yahi wajah thi ki jab Brahmin ne pehli baar Krupiri ko sach bataya, to use yakin nahi hua. Krupiri unhe itna maan deti thi ki agar Brahmin ki baat sunne ke baad Kripaldev use jhoot bolkar taal bhi dete, to wah maan leti. Agar Kripaldev bas itna keh dete, "Nahi Krupi, yah sab mithya hai," to Krupiri bina kisi aur sawal ke, ankh band karke unke charan chhookar maafi maang leti. Ek aise insan par sandeh karna jisne kabhi apne kartavya me kami nahi chhodhi, Krupiri ko apna sabse bada paap lagta. Parantu Kripaldev ne us din koi lag-lapet nahi ki. Dhriv ki kasam ke aage unhone poora sach ugal diya.

Isiliye yah aaghaat aur bhi gehra tha. Krupiri ne apne kaanpte hue aanchal se aansu poonche. Usne apni siskhiyon ko dabaya aur bade hi maryadit, parantu toote hue swar me poocha, "Hey Nath... mujhe bas yah bata dijiye. Kya aap hamare sath apna aage ka jeevan vyateet karenge, ya us parvat me basne ki apki ichha hai?"

Is prashna ka uttar dete hi Kripaldev ke saamne ek naya sankat khada ho gaya: Dhriv ka bhavishya. Kya Dhriv apne pita ke sath Kripal parvat jaana chahega, ya apni maa ke sath isi gaon me rehna chahega? 

Dhriv us samay mahez chhah varsh ka tha. Wah is bhaari faisle ko samajhne ke liye bahut chhota tha. Parantu jab uske saamne yah prashna rakha gaya, to balak ne bina soche apni maa ka pallu kas ke pakad liya. Shayad kisi anjaani samajh se, ya shayad isliye kyuki ek shishu ke liye maa ki mamta kisi bhi mahan pita se badi hoti hai.

Kripaldev, jinhe apna faisla mil chuka tha, bina koi kalesh kiye aur bina koi ansoo bahaye, ghar ki dehleez laangh gaye. Wah seedhe Kripal Ghati ki taraf nikal pade. Yah unke jeevan ka sabse bada nirnay tha. Ek stree, ek putra, aur saalon ki pratishtha chhodkar wah us prem ki taraf badh gaye jise samaj ne kabhi swikar nahi kiya tha. Is nirnay ne na keval ek pariwar ko toda, balki ek aisi bhayanak shrinkhala ki neev rakh di jo aane wali peedhi ko apne zeher me dubone wali thi.

Samay apni gati se aage badhta gaya. Dhriv, jo kabhi apne pita ki godi me baithkar veer kathayen suna karta tha, ab raat me apni maa ki godi me sar rakhkar Krishna ki kathayen sunne laga. Krupiri apne khud ke dukh ko dabate hue, apne bete ko yah kahaniyan sunati. Shayad is tarah wah apne bhitar ke khaali sthaan ko bharne ki koshish kar rahi thi. 

Dhriv ke liye yah kathayen keval kahaniyan nahi rahi; wah uski nayi prerna ban gayi. Usne apna vesh tak badal liya. Wah langot aur bansuri liye, sir par mor pankh sajaye, gaon ki galiyon me bilkul natkhat Krishna ki bhanti ghoomta. Wah gauon ke peechhe daudta, aur kabhi-kabhi kisi ke ghar se makkhan chura kar khaata. Gaon ka har balak use apna sabse priya mitra maanta tha. Dhriv ke andar ek aisi masoomiyat thi jo har kisi ko moh leti thi, parantu uski yah hasi, darasal uske andar ke pitraheen dukh ko chhupane ka ek tarika thi.

Gaon chhota tha, aur is chhote se gaon me sabse lachaar stree ab Krupiri thi. Sabhi use sahaanubhuti ki drishti se dekhte the. Aur shayad usi karan se Dhriv poore gaon ka laadla ban gaya tha. Parantu is gaon se thodi door ek aur gaon tha, jiske pass ek prachin Peepal ka ped tha. Us ped ke bare me kayi darawani kathayen prachalit thi. Kaha jata tha ki us ped ke neeche Kalahpriya (jise log Raja Kali bhi kaha karte the) ka raj chalta hai. Usi ped ke neeche jua khelna, madira peena, aur hinsa hona itni aam baat thi ki gaon ke bade-buzurg apne bachchon ko us taraf bhatakne tak nahi dete the.

Mai, Chandraprish, usi doosre gaon me raha karta tha. Mere pita ek kumhaar the. Mitti ke bartan banana unka pushtaini kaam tha. Mere dada ne badi mehnat se kuch zameen kharidi thi, jismein hum khoon-paseena bahakar fasal ugate the. Parantu mere pita ki ek buri aadat ne sab kuch barbaad kar diya. Unhe jue (gambling) ka aisa nasha chadha ki unhone dheere-dheere sari zameen us Peepal kalari me gawa di. Hamara ghar bhookh se jhoojhne laga. Us mushkil ghadi me humari sahayata meri mausi, Krupiri ne ki thi. Wah humein rashan aur kapda deti. Dhriv aur mai isi rishte ki wajah se bhaiyon jaise ban gaye. 

Jab ek baar Dhriv mere hi ghar thehra hua tha, hum chhat par aasman ke taaron ko ginte hue raat bhar baatein kar rahe the. Na jaane kaise, baaton-baaton me maine use us Peepal ke ped ke bare me sab kuch bata diya. 

"Wahan log raat bhar jua khelte hai, Dhriv," maine uski aankhon me kutuhal dekhte hue kaha. "Madira ki nadiyan behti hai, aur log nashe me apna hosh kho baithte hai." 

Mujhe us samay andaza nahi tha ki yah jankari Dhriv ke andar kaisi aag lagayegi. Dhriv swabhaav se hi upadravi tha. Jo bhi naya sunta, use apni ankhon se dekhne ki ichha usme jaag jaati thi.

Agli shaam, bina kisi ko bataye, Dhriv akela hi us Peepal ke ped ki taraf nikal gaya. Krupiri mausi ne uski parvarish bade pyar se ki thi. Unhone Dhriv ko sikhaya tha ki 'sahi' kya hai, parantu yah nahi bataya ki 'galat' kya hota aur usse door kyun rehna chahiye. Unhe darr tha ki galat cheezon ka zikra karne se bachche me jigyasa jaag sakti hai. Parantu is chuppi ne ulta asar dikhaya. Jab Dhriv ko us anjaan duniya ka pata chala, to wah khincha chala gaya.

Peepal kalari ke log hamesha naye shikar ki talaash me rehte the. Jab unhone Dhriv jaise ek sundar, anjaan balak ko dekha, to unhone use gher liya. Unhone haskar uske haath me madira ki ek botal thama di. 

Dhriv, jise pata hi nahi tha ki yah teekhi mehek wali cheez kya hai, use bade kutuhal se soongh raha tha. Kismat achhi thi ki mai use dhoondhta-dhoondhta theek usi samay wahan pahunch gaya. Jab maine use us bhid ke beech paya, to mera dil chhati faadkar bahar aane ko ho gaya. Maine aage badhkar uska haath pakda aur use wahan se kheench nikala. Parantu thodi der ho chuki thi; Dhriv us madira ke nashe me halka sa jhoomne laga tha.

Mai kisi tarah use chhupate hue apni chhat par le gaya aur chupchaap khatiya par sula diya. Us raat mai aankh khol kar jaagta raha. 

Agli subah, jab usne apni bhaari aankhen kholi, to maine use kandhe se pakda. "Dhriv, yah sab tumhare liye theek nahi hai!" maine gusse aur dar me kaha. "Mausi ne tumhe kitne pyaar se pala hai. Agar unhe pata chala ki tum madira peete ho, to unka toota hua dil poori tarah bikhar jayega." 

Dhriv ka sir bhari tha. Wah chupchaap zameen ghoorta raha. Jate samay maine use dobara chetawani di ki wahan laut kar kabhi mat jaana.

Parantu kismat ko kuch aur hi manzoor tha. Kuch mahino baad, maine Dhriv ko wapas usi Peepal kalari me dekha. Pehli baar me to mai use pehchan hi nahi paya. Uske hathon me bansuri nahi thi, aur sir se mor pankh gaayab tha. Uski aankhen laal theen aur kapde mitti me sane the. Mujhe pata chala ki wah pichle kayi dino se yahan aakar sharab pi raha tha.

Mera andar tak kaanp utha. Ek hansta-khelta balak itni jaldi is daldal me kaise dhans gaya? Dhriv mujhe dekhte hi rone laga. Usne nashe me ladkhadate hue apne haath meri taraf badhaye. "Chandika... oo priya Chandriya... Chanduka..." 

"Mera naam Chandraprish hai!" maine gusse me uska haath jhatka aur use apne kandhe ka sahara dekar chalne ko kaha.

Us andhere, pathreele raste par chalte hue Dhriv achanak ruka. Usne poori taaqat se meri baanh pakad li. Uski laal, sooji hui aankhon me ek bhayanak khauff tha. 

"Chandika, sun..." uski aawaz kaanp rahi thi. "Jab bhi mai peeta hoon na... tab kuch na kuch bura zaroor hota hai."

Maine uski baat par hansi udayi. Mujhe laga madira ne uski buddhi bhramit kar di hai. Parantu Dhriv ne rote hue meri kameez kas kar pakad li. "Mera vishwas kar Chandika! Pichle hafte jab mai pehli baar us kalari me gaya tha... agle hi din maa ka haath chulhe me buri tarah jal gaya tha. Aur parso? Parso jab maine dobara pi, to meri sabse pyari bachhiya, jiske sath mai dhoop me khelta tha, achanak mar gayi!" Uski laal aankhon me itna sachha khauff tha ki meri hansi wahin thehar gayi. Mera hriday bhay se bhar gaya. Kya yah sach me sanyog tha, ya kisi bhayanak rahasya ki aahat?

Mai Dhriv ko uske ghar surakshit chhodkar wapas lauta, par us raat mujhe neend nahi aayi. Andhere chhat par lete hue mai bas yahi sochta raha: Kya is duniya me waqai aisi koi shakti hoti hai jo kisi ke apraadh ka dand is tarah, itni nishthurta se deti hai? Aur agar haan... to kya Dhriv apne us andhkaar ki taraf badh raha tha jismein uska pita varshon pehle kho gaya tha?

## Adhyay 2 — Shrap ka Pehla Pramaan

Us raat ke baad, jab maine Dhriv ko uske ghar tak surakshit pahuncha diya, mera man ek ajeeb uljhan me pada raha. Mujhe samajh nahi aa raha tha ki maine jo dekha aur suna, use kis tarah samjha jaaye. Kya yah sach me koi shraap tha, ya sirf ek dukhi balak ki bhram bhari kalpana? 

Is prashna ka uttar dhoondhne ke liye maine apni maa ka sahara liya. Meri maa, Krupiri mausi ki sabse gehri saheli thi. Jab unhone mere chehre par chinta bhaanp li, tab unhone mere kandhe par haath rakha. 

"Kya baat hai beta? Tu itna pareshan kyun lag raha hai?" unhone poocha.

Maine unhe sach-sach bata diya. Maine bataya ki Dhriv ne mujhse kya kaha tha, aur mera dil bhay se kyun kaanp raha hai.

Meri maa ne ek lambi saans li. Unki aankhon me ek purani yaad ki chamak aayi, aur unka chehra gambhir ho gaya. "Beta," unhone dhimi aawaz me kaha, "mai tumhe wah baat bataungi jo maine varshon pehle Krupiri ke munh se suni thi. Parantu tumhe iska zikra kisi se nahi karna hai, khaaskar Dhriv se. Agar usne yah sun liya, to wah khud ko poori tarah tod dega."

Maine unhe vachan diya. Tab meri maa ne mujhe wahi kahani sunayi jo maine tumhe pehle batayi thi—Kripaldev aur Raadhna ki, unki jhoothi kasam ki, aur Krupiri ke toote hue vishwas ki. Ant me unhone ek aisi baat kahi jisne meri ragon me khoon jama diya. 

"Beta, jis raat Kripaldev ne pehli baar Raadhna ke saath madira pi thi, theek usi raat unka ghar Krupiri se hamesha ke liye toot gaya tha," meri maa ne apne haath jodd kar aasmaan ki taraf dekha. "Tab se yah baat gaon ki kuch purani striyon me chupke-chupke kahi jaati hai. Jab bhi us parivaar ka koi vyakti madira ko haath lagata hai, ek anarth avashya hota hai. Log ise 'Kripal ka shraap' kehte hai."

Yah sunkar meri rooh kaanp gayi. Agar yah sach tha, to iska seedha matlab yah tha ki Dhriv ne bhi theek wahi paap kar diya tha jise kayi saalon pehle uske pita ne kiya tha. Bas Dhriv is bhayanak sach se poori tarah anjaan tha. Ek nirdosh balak ne anjaane me hi usi aag me pair rakh diya tha jisne kabhi uske ghar ko jala kar raakh kiya tha.

"Parantu maa, yah to ghor anyay hai!" maine aakrosh me poocha. "Kya Bhagwan us masoom ko us galti ki itni bhari saza de rahe hai jiske anjaam se wah poori tarah anjaan tha?" 

Meri maa ne udaas hokar sir hilaya. "Beta, sansaar ke niyam hamesha samajh me nahi aate. Kabhi-kabhi anjaane me jalayi gayi aag bhi poora ghar fook deti hai."

Maine faisla kiya ki Dhriv ko yah baat batani hi hogi. Use apni sthiti ka gyaan hona chahiye, taaki wah khud ko sambhal sake. Ek sham jab Dhriv mere ghar aaya, uski aankhon me thakan aur udaasi thehri hui thi. Maine use baithaya aur us 'shraap' ke baare me sab kuch bata diya. 

Uski pratikriya ajeeb thi. Pehle uski aankhon me avishwas jhalka, jaise use lag raha ho ki mai koi mazak kar raha hoon. Phir uske chehre par krodh utar aaya—shayad apne pita ke prati, ya shayad khud apne bhagya ke prati. Parantu ant me us krodh ki jagah ek aisi udaasi ne le li jisse mai pehle kabhi rooba-ru nahi hua tha. Wah toot chuka tha.

"Chandika, agar yah sach hai, to mai kya karu?" usne apna sir dono hathon me girate hue poocha. "Mujhe to pata hi nahi tha ki madira peena mere liye maut ko bulana hai! Gaon me to sabhi log peete hai. Mujhe bhi laga ki mai thoda sa pi lu, kya harj hai? Mujhe kya pata tha ki mera ek ghoont mere pita ke us shraap ko wapas zinda kar dega? Jab mujhe is aag ka pata hi nahi tha, to mai khud ko jalne se kaise bachata?" 

Uski is baat ka mere paas koi santoshjanak uttar nahi tha. Yahi is shraap ka sabse bhayanak pehlu tha—isne Dhriv par tab waar kiya tha jab wah apne hi ateet se anjaan tha. Jab tak use is darr ka ehsaas hua, tab tak uska bahut kuch bikhar chuka tha.

Us din ke baad, Dhriv ne apne aap se ek drdh wada kiya. Usne madira poori tarah chhodne ka sankalp liya, parantu zeher itni aasani se atma ko mukt nahi karta. Agle kuch hafte uske liye kisi bhayanak narak se kam nahi the. Raat ke andhere me uske haath sookhe patto ki tarah kaanpte the. Nashe ki us zalim talab me wah bistar par gote khata, thande paseene se bheeg jata, aur kai baar to apni hi naso ko nochne lagta. Use samajh me aane laga tha ki yah lat kisi daanav ki tarah uske khoon me baith chuki thi jise zinda rehne ke liye ab madira chahiye thi. 

Phir bhi, us shraap ke bhayanak darr aur apni drdh ichha shakti ke sahare, usne kayi mahino tak madira ki ek boond ko bhi honth nahi lagaya. Dheere-dheere uski jaddojahad kam hui aur gaon me sab kuch shanti se chalne laga. Uski maa, Krupiri ne varshon baad rahat ki saans li. Dhriv ek baar phir se wahi purana natkhat balak banne ki koshish kar raha tha. Wah phir se bansuri bajata, gaon ke bachchon ke sath khelta. Uski hasi ek baar phir gaon ki galiyon me goonjne lagi thi. Mujhe bhi lagne laga ki shayad usne apni niyati ko hara diya hai.

Parantu jaisa ki har kahani me hota hai, sukh zyada der tak nahi tikta. Gaon me ek naya vyapari aaya. Wah door des se apne sath sharab ki nayi kismein, naye rang, nayi mithaiyan, aur naye vastra laya tha. Poore gaon me utsav ka mahaul chha gaya. Gaon ke ek dhanaadhya kisan ke ghar vivah samaroh tha, jahan sabko nimantran mila. Dhriv bhi wahan apne doston ke sath gaya. Un doston ko abhi yah nahi pata tha ki Dhriv kya vachan le chuka hai.

Samaroh me jab madira ka daur chala, Dhriv ke doston ne hasi-mazak me use ek pyaala pakda diya. "Are Dhriv! Aaj ke din to pi le. Ek ghoont se kya hoga? Tu to hamesha bhaagta rehta hai. Aaj to khushi ka mauka hai!" 

Dhriv ne bahut mana kiya, parantu doston ki zidd aur samajik dabav ke aage wah kamzor pad gaya. Ek aisi mehfil jahan na peena ek ajeeb baat maani jati thi, wahan Dhriv khud ko rok nahi paya. Usne kaanpte haathon se wah pyaala liya, aur ek hi saans me use dhalak gaya.

Us raat, jab sab log vivah ki khushiyon me doobkar so gaye, tab theek subah ke chaar baje (4:00 AM) gaon ke sabse buzurg vyakti—Baba Mangaldas, jinhone Dhriv ko apne kandhon par baithakar poora gaon ghumaya tha aur use saggay pote se badhkar pyaar kiya tha—unka achanak dehant ho gaya. Bina kisi bimari, bina kisi aahat ke, unhone saans lena band kar diya tha. Jaise hi gaon me yah manhoos khabar aag ki tarah faili, Dhriv ki rooh tak kaanp uthi. Uski himmat hi nahi hui ki wah us aangan tak jaaye aur apne Baba ka mrit chehra dekh sake. Uske andar aisi bhayanak aatm-glani aur darr bhar gaya tha ki uski saansein atakne lagin.

Subah ke theek saade-paanch (5:30 AM) baj rahe the. Aasman me abhi ujaala bhi nahi hua tha ki Dhriv bhaagta hua mere aangan me aaya. Uski haalat dekhkar mera dil baith gaya. Wah aadha nashe me tha, aadha bhayanak gam me dooba hua tha, aur uski bachi-kuchi atma hosh me aate hi chhatpata rahi thi. Uski aankhen khun ki tarah laal theen aur kapde mitti me sane hue the. Wah cheekhta hua mere pairo me gir pada. Usne zameen ko apne naakhuno se kuredte hue hichkiyon ke beech kaha, "Chandika! Mujhe laga tha yah sab ek kalpana thi... mujhe laga tha maine narak ko hara diya hai! Parantu us shraap ne ek masoom jaan le li... Baba Mangaldas... unhone kisika kya bigada tha Chandika? Wah to mujhe sabse zyada pyaar karte the! Unke lahu ka daag mere in hathon par hai jinse maine kuch ghante pehle wah madira pi thi! Maine unhe maar dala! Sirf maine!" Lagatar zameen khuredne ke karan Dhriv ki dason ungliyan khoon se lathpath ho chuki theen, parantu use us sharirik dard ka ab koi ehsaas hi nahi ho raha tha.

Maine kaanpte hue haathon se uske kandhe pakde aur use seene se laga kar samjhane ki koshish ki, "Dhriv... chup ho ja mere bhai! Yah keval sanyog ho sakta hai! Baba ki umar bhi ho chuki thi." Parantu meri apni aawaz me koi vishwas nahi tha. Mere bhitar bhi wahi atoot darr baith chuka tha. Andar hi andar, mai khud bhi jaan gaya tha ki humne aazaadi nahi, maut ko gale lagaya tha.

Is ghatna ke baad Dhriv ka swabhav poori tarah badal gaya. Jo balak kabhi sabka priya tha, jo har aangan me hasi bikhera karta tha, wah ab andhere me akele rehna pasand karne laga. Use darr lagne laga ki agar wah kisi samaroh me gaya, to koi na koi use madira dene ki zidd karega. Aur agar usne mana kiya, to log use ajeeb, ghamandi, ya asamajik samjhenge. Is dwidha ne use andar hi andar khokhla karna shuru kar diya. Wah raaton ko akela chhat par baithkar aasman ko takta rehta, jaise wahan koi aisa uttar dhoondh raha ho jo use is duniya me kahin nahi mil raha tha.

Krupiri mausi ne dekha ki unka beta badal raha hai. Uski hasi gayab ho gayi thi. Wah pehle jaisa natkhat nahi raha. Parantu unhe iska sahi karan kabhi pata nahi chala, kyuki Dhriv ne yah raaz sirf mujhse baataya tha. Mujhe bhi darr tha ki agar mai Krupiri mausi ko sach bataunga, to log Dhriv ko hi doshi thehra denge, jabki asli galti to unki thi jinhone use zabardasti pilaya. Is darr ne mujhe chup rehna sikha diya. Aaj jab mai peeche mudkar dekhta hoon, to mujhe lagta hai ki yahi meri sabse badi bhool thi. Mera maun... shayad Dhriv ki takleef ko aur bhi gehra kar gaya.

Baba Mangaldas ki maut ne Dhriv ko andar tak tod diya tha. Usne us din se madira se poori tarah naata tod liya. Mahino tak usne gaon ke kisi bhi utsav ya bheed-bhad wale aayojan me jaana band kar diya. Parantu jab raat hoti, to andhere me use Baba ka muskurata hua, aur phir achanak mrit pada hua chehra dikhayi deta. Us bhayanak aatm-glani aur akelepan ki peeda me, uski raaton ki neend poori tarah ud chuki thi. Wah ghanto bistar par chhatpata rehta, jaise koi atma use andar hi andar kha rahi ho.

Ek raat, jab uski ghutan bardasht se bahar ho gayi, wah utha aur usne ek purani chhupayi hui botal nikal li. Uske haath buri tarah kaanp rahe the. Usne dhakkan khola aur botal ko apne hontho ke kareeb laya. Parantu theek usi pal, uske dimaag me Baba Mangaldas ka wah sota hua, thanda chehra ghoom gaya. Dhriv ki rooh kaanp gayi. Usne jaldi se dhakkan band kiya aur botal ko wapas sandook me phek diya. Wah is narak me jalne ko taiyar tha, par kisi aur ki jaan lene ko nahi.

Parantu nashe ka daanav itni aasani se haar nahi maanta. Agle kayi ghanton tak uske bhitar ek bhayanak yuddh chalta raha. Raat aur bhi andheri aur lambi hoti ja rahi thi. Jab akelepan ka shor uske kano ke parde faadne laga, to Dhriv ne phir se wah botal nikaal li. Is baar uski aankhon me aansu the aur uske andar ka pagalpan haawi ho chuka tha. Par jaise hi madira ki mehak uski naak tak pahunchi, Baba ki chita ki aag uski aankhon ke saamne naach uthi. "Nahi!" Dhriv cheekh pada. Usne poori taqat se wah botal deewar par maar kar tod di. Kanch ke tukde aur madira poore kamre ke farsh par bikhar gaye. Usne apne dono haathon se apna sir pakda aur wahin kone me baithkar bachchon ki tarah rone laga. Use laga usne nashe ko hara diya hai.

Parantu insaan ka dhairya kisi lohe ki zanjeer jaisa nahi hota ki kabhi na toote. Kuch hi ghanton baad, usi raat ke pichhle pehar me, jab ek bhayankar barsaat shuru hui, Dhriv ki zinda rehne ki zidd ne apna aakhri hathiyar daal diya. Uski mansik taqat poori tarah dhah gayi. Wah zameen par ghutno ke bal rengta hua us farsh tak gaya jahan madira bikhri hui thi. Usne kanch ke tukdon ke beech apni hatheliyan ragad kar wah zameen par faili madira ko ikattha kiya. Kanch ke nukeelay tukde uski hatheliyon ko cheerte hue andar ghus gaye. Uske khoon ne us bikhri hui madira ko laal kar diya, parantu use us sharirik dard ka ehsaas tak nahi hua. Usne rote hue, kaanpte haathon se us khoon-sani madira ko chat liya. Yah uski drdh ichha shakti ki sabse buri, sabse ghinouni haar thi. Sirf ek raat ki neend paane ke bhram me, usne us zeher ko apne gale se neeche utaar liya.

Aur us zeher ne ek baar phir apna asar dikhaya. Usi raat jab moosladhar barish aayi, to Dhriv ke ghar ki chhat, jo varshon se mazboot khadi thi, ek bhayanak aawaz ke sath achanak dhah gayi. Mitti aur eent ka dher theek usi khatiya par aakar gira jahan Dhriv soya karta tha. Khushkismati se Krupiri mausi us waqt padosi ke ghar thin, aur Dhriv nashe me bahar aangan me gira hua tha, isliye dono bach gaye. Parantu us ghar ka theek uske sote hue sthaan par tootna, Dhriv ke liye maut ki ek khuli chetavani ban gaya.

Usne us malbe ko dekhte hue, baarish me bheegte hue mujhse kaha, "Chandika, yah ghar nahi... yah meri bachi-kuchi rooh tooti hai. Mujhe lagta hai mera wajood tabhi tak surakshit hai jab tak mai shudh rahu. Jis din mai apni maryada phir todunga, us din meri jad hi ukhad jayegi."

Yah sunkar mujhe laga ki Dhriv ab is shraap ko apne poore hriday se maan chuka tha. Ek insan jo apni niyati ko is tarah swikaar kar leta hai, uske andar ek ajeeb si shunyata aa jaati hai. Na wah poori tarah is niyati ke khilaaf ladta hai, na hi wah poori tarah haar maan kar apne aap ko chhod deta hai. Wah bhay ke maare beech me kahin latak jata hai. Aur yahi latakna, yahi darr ka aatank, Dhriv ke aage ke jeevan ka sabse bada bojh ban gaya.

## Adhyay 3 — Shivani aur Toota Hua Bharosa

Samay ke saath-saath Dhriv aur mai dono jawan hone lage. Mai apne pita ki chhooti hui zameen ko sambhalne ki koshish kar raha tha. Nayi gili mitti ko apne hathon se aakar dena aur chaak chalana seekh raha tha. Aur Dhriv? Wah apne us anokhe, adrishya bojh ko akela dhota raha. 

Uski umar bees se baais varsh ki hone ko aayi. Is umar me zindagi jin naye rango se milti hai, Dhriv bhi unse anjaan nahi raha. Humare gaon me har varsh ek bada mela laga karta tha. Door-door se log apni bailgaadiyon me saman leke aate the. Pashuon ki khareed-farokht hoti thi, aur raat dhalte hi dhol ki thaap par poora gaon jhoom uthta tha.

Us mele me hi Dhriv ki mulakat ek ladki se hui. Jiska naam tha Shivani. Wah kisi door ke gaon se apne mama ke ghar chhutti bitane aayi thi. Shivani ka roop aisa tha ki jo bhi use ek baar dekh le, uski nazar wahin thehar jaye. Uski aankhon me ek chanchal chamak thi, jaise koi hiran kasturi ki khoj me van me daud raha ho. 

Jab Dhriv ki nazrein pehli baar Shivani par padi, wah mele ke ek konay me apni saheliyo ke sath chudiyan khareed rahi thi. Dhriv, jo aksar chup-chap aur udaas rehta tha, us din pehli baar khulkar muskuraya. Mai theek uske paas khada tha. Maine uski aankhon me ek aisi chamak dekhi jo varshon se lapata thi. Mujhe laga, shayad yahi wah khushi hai jo Dhriv ke purane ghaav ko bhar degi. Shayad yah prem use us shraap ke bojh se thodi rahat dilayega.

Bade hi sankoch ke saath, Dhriv ne aage badhkar Shivani se baat karne ki himmat juta li. Unki pehli baatcheet un kanch ki chudiyon ke rang ko lekar hui thi. 

"Tumhe kaunsi zyada pasand aayi?" Shivani ne haskar uski taraf ek laal aur ek hari chudi badhate hue poocha. "Laal wali, ya hari wali?"

Dhriv thoda ghabra gaya, par usne jaldi se khud ko sambhala. Uski taraf dekhte hue usne dhimi aawaz me kaha, "Mujhe... mujhe wah rang pasand hai jo tumhari muskurahat ke sath jache." 

Yah sunkar Shivani ki palke jhuk gayin aur wah sharma kar has padi. Wahi pehli mulakat unki dosti ki neev ban gayi. Shivani jab tak apne mama ke ghar rahi, dono roz milte. Kabhi nadi kinare, to kabhi khet ki medh par. Dhriv use apni bansuri bajakar sunata, aur Shivani apne meethhe swar me gaon ki kahaniyan sunati.

Shivani ko Dhriv ke shraap ke bare me kuch bhi nahi pata tha. Aur Dhriv bhi nahi chahta tha ki wah is sach ko jaane. Use bhay tha ki agar Shivani ko pata chala ki Dhriv ke madira chhute hi anarth hota hai, to wah use shraapit samajh kar door bhaag jayegi. Isliye usne yah raaz apne seene me daba kar rakha. Keval mai hi tha jise yah sara sach pata tha. Kabhi-kabhi, jab Shivani aas-paas hoti, to Dhriv meri taraf aisi nazron se dekhta jaise ek maun prarthana kar raha ho: *"Kripya mera sach mat batana, Chandraprish."*

Samay ke sath unki dosti ek gahre, atoot prem me badal gayi. Shivani Dhriv ke jeevan ki sabse sundar roshni ban chuki thi. Wah uske liye kisi taweez jaisi thi; jab bhi Dhriv ke bhitar nashe ka wahi purana daanav jaagne lagta, wah Shivani ki muskurahat yaad kar leta aur uski aatma shant ho jaati. Shivani ne anjaane me hi Dhriv ko us bhayanak bhay se nikaal liya tha. Un dono ke prem ke anokhe pal—jaise baarish me ek hi patte ke neeche chhipna, ya purnima ki raat me ek-doosre ka haath pakad kar ghanto aasmaan takna—Dhriv ko yakeen dilane lage the ki ab uska jeevan kisi shraap ke adheen nahi hai.

Is tarah ek lamba samay guzar gaya. Dhriv ne madira ki parchhai ko bhi khud par nahi padne diya. Parantu niyati ek aise jaal bun rahi thi jiska unhe andaza bhi nahi tha.

Ek din, gaon walon ki nazron se chhupkar, Dhriv aur Shivani paas ke ek pahadi mele me ghoomne nikal gaye. Unhe laga tha ki wapas aane se pehle kisi ko bhanak nahi lagegi. Parantu shaam dhalte hi mausam achanak bigad gaya. Bhayankar barfili hawaein chalne lagin aur raste band ho gaye. Unhe majbooran ek sunsan, tooti hui pahaadi kothri me aashray lena pada.

Raat badhne ke sath thand itni jaanlewa ho gayi ki Shivani ka shareer barf ki tarah thanda padne laga. Wah thithurte hue behosh hone lagi. Dhriv ghabra gaya. Usne aag jalane ki poori koshish ki, parantu lakdiyan geeli theen. Shivani ki saansein dheemi padne lagi theen. Theek usi waqt, us kothri me kisi pichhle yatri dwara chhodi gayi kanch ki ek botal Dhriv ki nazron me padi, jisme thodi madira bachi thi. 

Log kehte the ki madira khoon me garmi paida karti hai. Dhriv ke paas aur koi raasta nahi tha. Shivani ki jaan bachane ki aakhri koshish me, usne darrte-darrte wah botal uthayi. Usne thodi si madira Shivani ke hontho par ragdi, aur bachi hui madira khud pee li taaki uske khud ke khoon me garmi aaye aur wah apne shareer ki garmi se Shivani ko thand se bacha sake. Us waqt uske man me koi nasha nahi tha, keval apni premika ki jaan bachane ki fikar thi. Us zeher ko gale se utaarte waqt usne socha, "Agar aaj iske badle mujhe narak me bhi jalna pade, to mai taiyar hoon."

Dhriv ka prayas safal raha. Subah hote-hote Shivani ki saansein samanya ho gayin. Jaise hi mausam saaf hua, dono jaldi se gaon ki taraf laute. Parantu gaon me unka swagat us aag ne kiya jiski Dhriv ko ummeed nahi thi.

Unke raat bhar gayab rehne ki khabar poore gaon me fail chuki thi. Jaise hi unhone gaon me kadam rakha, Shivani ke pita aur unke aadmiyon ne Dhriv ko gher liya. Unhone Dhriv ko bedardi se peeta. Dhriv maar khata raha, par usne muh se ek shabd nahi nikala. Usi shaam, Shivani ke pita ne achanak uska vivah gaon ke us paar wale gaon ke sabse dhanaadhya Pradhan ke bete se tay kar diya. Aur yah vivah isi hafte, theek do din baad hona tay hua tha.

Dhriv ke pairon tale zameen khisak gayi. Uska dil itne jor se dhadak raha tha jaise phat jayega. Shraap ne apna kaam kar diya tha. Is baar kisi ki maut nahi hui thi, kisi ka ghar nahi gira tha. Is baar shraap ne wah ek cheez chheen li thi jisne Dhriv ko zinda rakha hua tha.

## Adhyay 4 — Shraap ki Jeet aur Nashe ki Zanjeer

Dhriv andha hokar Shivani ke mama ke ghar ki taraf bhaga. Wah raste me girtate-padte wahan pahuncha. Uski ankhon me ansoo the aur kapde dhool me sane the. Raat ki wah bachi-kuchi madira ki bhap ab bhi uski saanson aur pasine se uth rahi thi. 

Usne rote hue sabke saamne haath jod kar Shivani ko mangne ki koshish ki. "Mai Shivani se prem karta hoon! Kripya uski shadi us Pradhan ke bete se mat kijiye! Mai mar jaunga!"

Parantu us bhari sabha me Shivani ke pita ne use dhakke maar kar aangan se nikaal diya. "Tumhe sharm nahi aati?" unhone gusse me thookte hue kaha. "Kal raat meri beti ko bhaga kar le gaya, aur ab yahan aa kar meri izzatdar ghar ki beti par apna adhikaar jama raha hai? Teri saanson se saaf sharab ki badboo aa rahi hai, ghatiya insaan! Ek sharaabi, nashedi nithalle ko hum apni beti denge? Dur hato yahan se, warna kutton se nochwa doonga!"

Shivani andar se rote hue aayi, parantu jab usne Dhriv ko us dasha me dekha, aur uske paas se aati hui madira ki durghandh ko soongha, to uska bhi dil baith gaya. Usko laga Dhriv wahi aam sharaabi hai jo uski samajh aur pyar ki aukaat ke bahar tha. Usne chupchaap apni nazar feyr li. 

Wahi nazar feyrna Dhriv ke jeevan ka sabse aakhri dhakka tha.

Do din baad, jab Shivani ki doli gaon se guzri, to Dhriv kisi mritak ki tarah wahi khada raha. Shraap ne apna khel itni kroorta se khela tha ki ab Dhriv ke paas bachaane ke liye kuch bacha hi nahi tha. Usne jis insaan se sabse zyada prem kiya tha, madira ke usi ek ghoont ne us insan ko usse hamesha ke liye chheen liya.

Us din Dhriv poori tarah haar gaya. Usne samajh liya tha ki wah kabhi bhi is paash se nahi nikal payega. Ab uske bhitar ka bhay khatam ho chuka tha, kyuki bhay tab hota hai jab khone ke liye kuch bacha ho. Ab jab sab kuch lut hi chuka tha, to usne apne us shraap ko apnane ka nirnay le liya. 

Dhriv ab har roz peene laga. Kabhi thoda, to kabhi botlein khali kar deta. Aur kyuki ab koi turant saza (kisi ka pair tootna ya marna) nahi milti thi, use lagne laga ki ab aur kya bura ho sakta hai. Parantu mai saaf dekh sakta tha ki is 'azaadi' ne uski rooh ko khokhla karna shuru kar diya tha. Wah khud us shraap ka sabse bada shikaar ban gaya tha.

Kuch hi mahino me uske haath kaanpne lage the. Uski aankhen, jo kabhi khuli hui, ujjwal aur nirdosh hoti theen, ab har waqt laal aur sooji hui rehti theen. Uska shareer jo kabhi gathila tha, ab durbal aur theher sa gaya tha. 

Uski maa, Krupiri, apne bete me aa rahe is bhayanak badlaav ko dekh rahi theen. Parantu wah khud se jhoot bolti rahin. "Shayad gaon ke naye doston ka asar hai," wah khud ko samjhati. Unhe yah pata hi nahi tha ki yah wahi shraap tha jisne ab roop badal liya tha. Ab yah kisi anarth ke roop me kisi aur ko nahi maarta tha, balki yah 'lat' ban chuka tha jo chup-chap aur dhimi aanch par Dhriv ki atma ko raakh kar raha tha. Saza se zyada bhayanak lat hoti hai, kyuki wah dikhti nahi.

Ek din maine himmat karke Krupiri mausi se akele me baat karne ki koshish ki. 

"Mausi," maine unka haath pakadte hue kaha, "Dhriv ki tabiyat theek nahi lagti. Kya aapne uski ankhon par gaur kiya hai?"

Krupiri mausi ne thandi aah bhari. Udas aawaz me bolin, "Haan beta. Mai bhi dekh rahi hoon. Parantu jab bhi usse poochti hoon, wah chilla padta hai ki 'Mai theek hoon maa, chinta mat karo!' Mujhe samajh nahi aata ki uske andar kya chal raha hai. Kabhi-kabhi mujhe lagta hai... jaise wah theek apne pita ki tarah banta ja raha hai."

Yah sunkar mera dil chhalni ho gaya. Krupiri mausi ko abhi bhi Kripaldev aur is shraap ka sach theek se samajh nahi aaya tha. Aur mai khud unhe yah sach batane ki himmat nahi juta pa raha tha. Mujhe darr tha ki agar unhe pata chala ki unka beta, apne pita ke us bhayanak shraap ko apni naso me paal raha hai, to unka pehle se toota hua dil hamesha ke liye bikhar jayega.

## Adhyay 5 — Patra aur Parvat ki Yatra

Do varsh is tarah aur beet gaye. Dhriv ab poori tarah us zeher ka aadi ho chuka tha. Wah din ka zyada-tar samay ya to nashe me dhutt rehta, ya is chinta me bhatakta ki agla ghoont kahan se milega. Gaon ke log, jo kabhi use apna sabse priya balak maante the, ab uske theharte hi apne darwaze band kar lete. Kuch log thukrakar use "vinasht yuva" kehte, to kuch Krupiri mausi ko dekhkar thandi saans bharte: *"Bechari Krupiri... aisa honhaar beta pakar kya kiya iski kismat ne."*

Yah taane Krupiri mausi ke kano tak bhi pahunchte the. Unki tabiyat is nirasaha me din-pratidin girti gayi. Unka chehra, jispar kabhi ek tejaswi sundarta thi, ab gehri jhurriyon, thakan, aur sooji hui aankhon me chhip gaya tha.

Ek subah jab mai unke ghar unki khairiyat poochne gaya, to unhone bistar se uthne ki koshish ki. Unke haath buri tarah kaanp rahe the. Unhone apni purani sandookchi se ek maila-kuchaila, mudi hui chithhi nikali aur meri taraf badhai. Unki aankhon me na gussa tha, na dukh—keval ek aisi thakan thi jaise ab rone ke liye bhi ansoo bache hi na hon.

"Chandraprish beta," unki aawaz khokhli thi, "yah patra unke ek purane mitra ne bheja hai. Wah vyapar ke silsile me pahaadon ki taraf gaya tha." 

Unhone thoda ruk kar, diwar ka sahara lete hue kaha, "Dhriv ke pita... ab is duniya me nahi rahe. Unka dehant ho chuka hai."

Mera dil dhadakna bhool gaya. Kripaldev! Ek aisa naam jiske baare me maine itni kathayen suni theen, jisse kabhi shraddha aur kabhi ghrina judi hoti thi. Wah ab is sansaar me nahi the?

Krupiri mausi ne apne phate hue aanchal se ansoo poonche aur meri taraf dekhte hue bolin, "Shayad ab waqt aa gaya hai ki Dhriv apni jadon ko jaan le. Apne pita ke sach ko poora jaan le. Mujhe pata hai yah khabar use aur bhi todegi... parantu shayad usse wahan kuch aisa mil jaye jo use is bhayanak andhkaar se bahar nikal sake. Ek maa ka dil aisa hi hota hai beta. Wah apne bete ko aag me tapte dekh sakti hai, agar use pata ho ki aag ke us paar uski bimari ka ilaaj hai."

Maine wah patra le jakar Dhriv ko dikhaya. Jab usne dekha ki kaagaz par uske pita ka naam likha hai, uski pratikriya behad kadwi thi. Usne patra ko hawa me phenkte hue kaha, "Jis vyakti ne mujhe kabhi apnaya hi nahi! Jisne meri maa ko jeete-jee maar diya! Jisne meri poori zindagi apni ek 'galti' se barbaad kar di... uske pate ka mai kya karunga Chandika? Jala do ise!" 

Uski aawaz me saalon ka daba hua krodh, apmaan aur cheekhta hua dard tha.

Parantu jab usne dekha ki zameen par gire patra ke saath ek chhoti si bhoj-patra ki parchi bhi hai, jismein kisi ke kaanpte haathon se likhe aakhri shabd the, uske qadam thehar gaye. Dhriv ka gussa thoda thanda pada. Usne jhuk kar, kaanpte haathon se wah parchi uthayi. 

Us par likha tha: 
*"Mere putra Dhriv, jo bhi tumhare sath ho raha hai, uska akela karan mai hoon. Mai jaanta hoon ki tum shayad mujhe kabhi maaf nahi karoge, aur mujhe koi haq bhi nahi hai tumse maafi maangne ka. Parantu mai chahta hoon ki tum sach jaano. Kripal Ghati jakar Raadhna se milna. Wahi tumhe wah sach bata sakti hai jo mai khud, apni poori zindagi me, samajh nahi paya. Shayad wahi tumhe is bhayanak bojh se mukti ka raasta dikha sakti hai. Tumhara pita, Kripaldev."*

Ise padhte hi Dhriv ke hoth bheench gaye. Barson baad uski laal aankhon me ek nishchay ki chamak aayi. Uske chehre par krodh bhi tha, jigyasa bhi, aur ek bechain kar dene wali asha bhi. 

Usne meri aankhon me dekhte hue kaha, "Chandika, mujhe wahan jaana hoga. Chahe kuch bhi ho! Mujhe apne is shraap ki jad jaan-ni hai. Mujhe jaanna hai ki yah sach me koi daivik shraap hai... ya yah keval meri kamzori hai jise mai bahana banakar apni har galti ko chhupa raha hoon!"

Maine use akela nahi jaane diya. Mujhe pata tha ki yah yatra kitni lumbi aur jhakhzor dene wali hogi. Mujhe darr tha ki akele me wah ya to kisi anarth ka shikaar ho jayega, ya dukh me doobkar wapas us botal ki taraf mud jayega. Hum dono ne saath me Kripal Ghati ki taraf yatra shuru ki. Humare kandhon par thoda sa ration tha, ek chhota sa kambal, aur dimaag me hazaron sawalon ka bhaari bojh.

Raste bhar Dhriv zyadatar chup raha. Parantu kabhi-kabhi uske honth khud hi khud hilne lagte, jaise wah apne pita ke bhoot se jhagad raha ho. Kabhi wah achanak ruk kar aasmaan ki taraf dekhta aur cheekhta, "Pita ji, aapne aisa kyun kiya? Agar aapko Raadhna se hi prem karna tha, to mujhe janm dene se pehle hi yah faisla kyun nahi liya? Mujhe is duniya me hi kyun laaye jab aapko pata tha ki aapka rasta wahan khatam hota hai?!" 

Mujhe uski yah dasha dekhkar rona aata tha. Jo balak kabhi hothon par bansuri rakhkar pahaadon ki hawa me sangeet ghol deta tha, wah aaj apne hi ateet ke bhoot se, akela, jadd-ojehad kar raha tha.

Parvaton ke raste lambe aur kathin the. Hum ghane jangalon se guzre, jahan sookhe patton ki awaazein darr paida karti theen. Humne aisi nadiyan paar keen jinka barfeela paani pairon me chubhta tha. Raat ko jab hum kisi anjaan gaon me theharte, to Dhriv kisi bujurg se apne pita ka naam poochta. Kuch unhe pehchante, kuch anjaan the. 

Jaise-jaise hum upar, aur upar chadhte gaye, hawa thandi aur saaf hoti gayi. Badal theek humare sir ke upar mandraane lage, maano hum aasman me chal rahe hon. Prakriti ka yah vishal roop Dhriv ke dimaag ko thoda shaant kar raha tha. Jaise yah pahaad use khud us kathor sach ko sehne ke liye taiyar kar rahe hon jo use us choti par milne wala tha.

Ek raat, jab hum ek pahaadi gufa me aag jalakar thand se bachne ki koshish kar rahe the, Dhriv ne bhunne hue aloo meri taraf badhate hue kuch aisa kaha jise mai kabhi nahi bhoolunga.

"Chandika," aag ki roshni me uski aankhen nam theen. "Tumne mera itna saath diya... itne varshon tak. Bina kisi swarth ke, mere is shraap ka hissa bane rahe. Mujhe nahi pata mai tumhara yah rin kaise chukaunga."

Maine apna ansoo chhupane ke liye haskar baat taal di. "Pagal ho gaya hai? Tum meri mausi ke bete ho. Mera chhota bhai hai tu. Yah to mera farz hai!" 

Parantu andar hi andar, us andheri gufa me, mujhe ehsaas ho raha tha ki yah yatra sirf Dhriv ke liye nahi hai. Yah mere liye bhi utni hi mahatvapoorna thi. Mai apne mitra ke dukh ka boj utha kar, darasal zindagi ke sabse gehre rahasyon ki khoj kar raha tha.

## Adhyay 6 — Raadhna aur Tatastha Bhagwan ka Rahasya

Kayi din ki thaka dene wali yatra ke baad, aakhirkaar hum Kripal Ghati parvat ki tarai me pahunche. Wahan ka drishya kisi aur hi lok jaisa lagta tha. Chaaron taraf oonche Devdaar ke ped aasmaan ko chhoote the. Unke patton ke beech se chhan kar aati sooraj ki sunehri kiranein zameen par ajeeb-si aakritiyan bana rahi theen. Wahan ek gehri, pavitra khamoshi thi; bilkul us shor aur kolahal ke vipreet jise hum gaon me chhod aaye the. 

Pahaadi logo se rasta poochte-poochte hum ek chhote se aashram tak pahunche, jo theek parvat ki thandi chhaya me basa hua tha. Yahan wahi saadhvi rehti thi jinke baare me log shraddha se baatein karte the.

Jaise hi hum us baans ke dwar par pahunche, andar se ek vriddha saadhvi bahar aayin. Unki umar bhale hi dhal chuki thi aur baal barf ki tarah safed the, parantu unki aankhon me ek aisi thehri hui shanti thi jaisi maine poore jeevan me kisi me nahi dekhi thi. Jaise unhone zindagi ke sabhi tufaano ko seene par jhel liya ho, aur us peeda ke us paar aakar unhe wah sthirta mil gayi ho jo keval pahaadon ko milti hai.

Unhone mud kar dekha. "Kaun hai?"

"Mai... mai Dhriv hoon," Dhriv ki aawaz me ek ajeeb sa bhari-pan tha. 

Yah naam sunte hi us vriddha ke kadam wahin thehar gaye. Unki thehri hui aankhon me achanak ansoo tairne lage. Bina ek shabd kahe, wah aage badhin aur unhone dono baahon se Dhriv ko gale laga liya. Aise, jaise koi maa varshon baad apne khoye hue putra se mil rahi ho.

Unhone apni kaanpti hui aawaz me kaha, "Mai Raadhna hoon. Tumhare pita ne apni aakhri saanson tak bas tumhara hi naam liya tha, Dhriv. Unhone mujhse vachan liya tha ki jis din tum yahan aaoge, mai tumhe poora sach bataungi. Chahe jitne varsh bhi lag jaayein."

Dhriv, jo raste bhar apne seene me aag aur krodh lekar aaya tha, Raadhna ko dekhkar aur unki us pahaadi shanti ko mahsoos karke achanak pighal gaya. Uski aankhon me abhi bhi sawal the, kadwahat thi, par uske hoth band the. Raadhna ne bade prem se humein aashram ke andar baithaya. Apne haathon se humein pahaadi bhojan parosa. 

Bhojan ke baad, Dhriv apne bhitar ka poora gubaar rok nahi paya. Usne rote hue apne jeewan ka ek-ek sach, ek-ek peeda Raadhna ke saamne khol kar rakh di. Usne Baba Mangaldas ki maut, apne khud ke toote hue aangan, madira ki us bhayanak lat, aur Shivani ke hamesha ke liye usse chhin jane ka dard sab kuch bata diya. Raadhna shant bhaav se uski baatein sunti rahin, parantu unki thehri hui aankhon me Dhriv ki peeda ko dekhkar aasu jhalak aaye.

Jab Dhriv apni vyatha sunakar thoda shant hua, tab Raadhna ek chataai par hamare saamne baithi. Unhone lumbi saans li aur dhimi aawaz me bolna shuru kiya.

"Putra Dhriv, sabse pehle to mai tumse haath jod kar maafi maangna chahti hoon," unhone kaha. "Mai jaanti hoon ki mera aur tumhare pita ka sambandh, samaj ki drishti me ek bhayanak paap tha. Isne tumhari maa ka sunehra ghar toda. Isne tumhara masoom bachpan chheena. Is sab ke liye mai khud ko kabhi maaf nahi kar payi."

Wah thoda ruki, aur Dhriv ki aankhon me poori sachaai se dekhte hue aage bolin: "Parantu, jo mai tumhe ab batane ja rahi hoon, wah shayad tumhe yah samajhne me madad karega ki tumhare saath jo durbhagya ghat raha hai, uska mool (root) karan kya hai."

"Mausi... meri maa gaon me kehti hai ki yah 'Kripal ka shraap' hai," Dhriv ne bhari aawaz me tokte hue kaha.

"Nahi," Raadhna ne inkaar me sir hilaya. "Yah koi sadharan shraap nahi hai jaisa gaon ki striyan kuyen par baatein karti hai. Yah us 'Tatastha Bhagwan' (Neutral God) ka niyam hai. Ek aisa devta jo na kisi ka paksh leta hai, na vipaksh. Jiske haathon me sirf nyaya ka tarazu hota hai—bina kisi bhed-bhaav, aur bina kisi rishte ya maryada ki parwah kiye. Unka nyaya keval karm ki parakh karta hai, aur unke nyaya dene ka tarika hum jaise sadharan manushyon ki choti samajh se bahut pare hai."

Unhone samjhate hue kaha, "Jab tumhare pita ne pehli baar madira pikar apni pighalti buddhi me mujhse sambandh joda tha, tab unke us paap ka fal unhe turant nahi mila. Tumhe yah ajeeb lagega Dhriv, parantu yahi Tatastha Bhagwan ka niyam hai! Jab tak insan paap ke 'maya-jaal' me poori tarah, matwale ghode ki tarah dooba rehta hai, tab tak Bhagwan uska hisaab nahi lete. Wah use uski maya me hi nange paon daudne ke liye chhod dete hai."

"Kyun?" Dhriv ne bechaini se poocha.

"Kyuki us andhi maya me insan khud ko sudharne ke liye taiyar hi nahi hota. Agar us matwali halat me use saza di jaaye, to wah sudharne ke bajaye aur zyada vidrohi aur krodhit ho jata hai. Parantu..." Raadhna ki aawaz thehar gayi. "...Jis din insan us paap se, chahe kisi darr se ya kisi bhi karan se, bahar nikalne ki koshish karta hai... bas theek usi din, uske saare purane karmo ka poora lekha-jokha ek hi pal me uske saamne pahad bankar toot padta hai."

"Isiliye," unhone gehri saans lete hue aage kaha, "jab Kripaldev ne mujhse milne aana kam kiya, ya jab bhi unhone khud ko rokne ki koshish ki, tab-tab unke jeevan me bhayanak anarth hue. Aur yaad karo jis din tumhari maa ne unhe kasam dekar sach bulwaya tha, aur unhone apna ghar chhoda tha! Yah bhi ek tarah se unka us jhoot ki zanjeer ko todne ka ek naya kadam tha. Aur theek usi din unka bas-basaya ghar hamesha ke liye dhas gaya. Unka ek-ek sach, unka paap, unke chehre par aakar laga."

Dhriv ne apne ghutne thaam liye the. Uski aankhon me anginit sawal the. "Parantu Mausi!" usne rote hue, cheekhte hue kaha. "Yah niyam mujh par kaise laagu hua? Maine kya kiya tha? Maine to koi paap nahi kiya tha! Mai to bas apne pita ka anjaan putra tha! Mujhe kyun dand mil raha hai?"

Raadhna ki aankhon se ek ansoo gira. Unhone Dhriv ka thanda haath apne haathon me liya. "Beta... yahi sabse kathin aur zehreela sach hai jo mujhe tumhe batana hai. Jab kisi pariwar me koi bada, atyant gehra paap-bandhan bantaa hai, to uska zeher keval us vyakti tak seemit nahi rehta. Wah us pariwar ki dhaara me, us khoon ki naliyon me, agli peedhi tak behta hai! Jab tak koi us zanjeer ko poori taqat se todne ka saahas na kare. Aur Dhriv... tum hi us khoon ke, us bandhan ke agle vaahak (carrier) the. Isi karan, tumhare roop me wahi niyam dobara shuru hua."

Unhone meri taraf dekha, phir wapas Dhriv ki taraf. "Jab tak tum 'darr' ke maare madira peene se rukte rahe, tumhara wah darr se rukna hi ek dharmik karya (good deed) tha. Aur jab bhi tumne us maryada ko tod kar madira pi, tumhe turant fal mila! Jante ho kyun? Kyuki tum abhi bhi apne us darr ki 'maya' me fase the. Tum sudharne ke liye, darr ke karan, taiyar extreme the. Isliye Bhagwan tumhe har galti par turant chot dekar samjha rahe the taaki tum sambhal sako!"

"Par ab to chot nahi milti?" Maine dheere se tokte hue poochha.

Raadhna ne thandi saans li. "Haan. Jab Shivani ko khone ke baad tumhara 'shraap' achanak ruka, uska matlab yah bilkul nahi tha ki tumhare paap maaf ho gaye. Uska sirf itna matlab tha ki ab Bhagwan ne tumhe bhi 'maya' (illusion) me chhod diya hai! Jaisa unhone tumhare pita ko chhoda tha. Taaki ab tum darr se nahi, balki apni khud ki marzi aur buddhi se 'Sahi' aur 'Galat' ka chunav karo. Bina kisi chot ya saza ke bhay ke!"

Raadhna ki aawaz me ek bhayanak chetawani thi. "Aur yahi, Dhriv, zindagi ki sabse badi, sabse chhalne wali pariksha hai. Kyuki jab insan ko lagta hai ki sab kuch theek hai, koi saza nahi mil rahi, tab wahi chhut, wahi maya, uski sabse bhayanak shatru ban jaati hai. Achhai ki maya me andha hokar insan sochta hai, 'dekho, mai pi raha hoon aur kuch nahi toot raha!' aur wah doobta chala jata hai. Yahi tumhare sath ho raha tha. Jab tumhara darr chala gaya, to tumhari maryada bhi jal gayi. Tum azad nahi hue... tum poori tarah us lat ke ghulam ho gaye."

Dhriv, jo ab phoot-phoot kar ro raha tha, usne dono hathon se apna chehra chhupa liya. "To fir Mausi... is gol-gol chakra se bahar nikalne ka rasta kya hai? Kya mai hamesha isi tarah tadapta rahunga? Kya mujhe is narq se kabhi mukti nahi milegi?"

Raadhna uske paas aayin. Unhone uske sar par apna haath rakha. 

"Upaay tumhare hi bhitar hai putra, is parvat me ya kisi aashram me nahi. Jab tak tum Bhagwan ke darr se madira chhodne ki koshish karte rahoge, tab tak tum ek vyapari ki tarah sirf saudebaazi kar rahe ho. Tum burai ko isliye nahi chhodna chahte kyuki wah buri hai, balki isliye chhodte ho kyuki tumhe saza ka bhay hai! Jis din tum sachche hriday se, bina kisi darr ke, yah maan loge ki nasha apne aap me hi ek vinash hai—tumhare liye, tumhare pariwar ke liye, aur tumhari atma ke liye... tabhi tum is maya se azad hoge."

Wah ruki, aur unhone ek muskurahat ke saath akhri baat kahi. "Jis din tumhe iska koi fal na bhi mile, na koi saza, na koi puraskaar, parantu tum use isliye chhod doge kyuki wah 'khud me hi ek vinash' hai... us din Dhriv, tumhare is bhayanak shraap ka antt ho jayega. Asli swatantrata tabhi milti hai jab insan 'Bhalai' ko isliye chunta hai kyuki wah Bhalai hai, kisi darr ya lalach ke karan nahi."

## Adhyay 7 — Mukti

Hum dono Raadhna ke us pahaadi aashram me kayi din tak rahe. Dhriv chup-chap, bina koi bahas kiye, unki baaton ko ek sookhi zameen ki tarah apne bhitar utaarta raha. 

Us aashram ka jeevan bahut saral tha. Subah pahaadon ke suraj se pehle uthkar dhyan karna. Phir aashram ke chhote se baagiche me sookhe patte batorna. Sant-mahatmaon ki sangat me baithkar unki katha sunna, aur shaam dhalte hi parvat ki chotiyon par barf ko sunhara hote dekhna. Dhriv ke liye yah shanti bilkul nayi thi. Dheere-dheere, usne is thehrav me apne liye ek aisi raahat dhoondh li jo use gaon ki dhool bhari galiyon me, ya nashe ke ghutan me kabhi nahi mili thi.

Raadhna use roz kuch naya sikhatin. Kabhi dhyan ki vidhi, to kabhi Bhagwad Geeta ke shlok. Ek din chataai par baithkar unhone Dhriv se kaha, "Putra, Geeta me Krishna Arjun se kehte hai ki insan ko phal ki iksha kiye bina apna kartavya karna chahiye. Yaad hai? Jab tum bachpan me mor pankh lagakar Krishna ke vesh me ghoomte the? Tab tumhe kahan pata tha ki ek din tumhe khud Krishna ke is sabse kathin updesh ko apne jeevan me utaarna padega." 

Yah sunkar Dhriv ki aankhon me ek naya prakash chamka. Jaise use apne us masoom bachpan aur apne is dardnaak vartaman ke beech ek an-dekha, gehra dhaaga dikh gaya ho.

Ek raat, mujhe neend nahi aa rahi thi. Mai apne kaksh se nikal kar taaza hawa lene aangan me aaya. Maine dekha ki Dhriv aashram ke aangan me gade ek purane Peepal ke ped ke neeche dhyan me magan baitha tha. 

Wahi Peepal! Kisi ajeeb sanyog ki tarah, yah ped theek us 'Peepal Kalari' ki yaad dilata tha jahan se uski yah poori barbadi shuru hui thi. Parantu yah Peepal alag tha. Iski thandi chhaya me koi jua nahi khela jata tha. Koi pighalta hua nasha nahi behta tha. Yahan keval shanti thi, aur ek thehra hua dhyan tha.

Uske chehre par aaj ek aisi atoot shanti thi jo maine barson se nahi dekhi thi. Jab wah dhyan se utha, to usne mujhe apne paas aane ka ishara kiya. Mai uske barabar me zameen par baith gaya.

Usne pahaadon ki andheri chotiyon ki taraf dekhte hue, drdh swar me kaha, "Chandika, mujhe aaj aakhir samajh aa hi gaya."

"Kya samajh aa gaya, Dhriv?"

"Yahi... ki maine poori zindagi Bhagwan ke sath ek vyapari ki tarah vyavhaar kiya! Mai hamesha unke darr se achha banne ka natak karta raha. Mai yahi sochta raha ki jab tak saza ka darr hai, tab tak madira se door raho. Parantu maine yah kabhi nahi socha ki peena apne aap me kitna vinashkari hai! Mai unse saude (deals) kar raha tha. Jabki mujhe unse ek bhatke hue putra ki tarah, seedhe dil se, rote hue maafi maangni chahiye thi. Aur shraddha ke sath sudharne ka sankalp lena chahiye tha."

Wah ruka. Usne meri taraf dekha aur uski aankhon me aansu the. "Ab mai madira isliye nahi chhodunga ki mujhe shraap dobara pakad lega, ya isliye nahi ki koi anarth ho jayega. Mai isliye chhodunga kyuki yah aadat khud ek 'Burai' hai! Yah wahi burai hai jisne meri maa ka sunehra ghar toda! Jisne mujhse mera sabse bada prem, Shivani ko chheen liya! Jisne Baba Mangaldas ki jaan li! Jisne mujhe mere hi wajood se door kar diya! Agar Bhagwan ab mujhe iske badle me kuch na bhi dein, koi shanti na dein, tab bhi mai is zeher ko kabhi nahi chhuunga. Kyuki ab mai jaanta hoon ki 'chhodna' hi Sahi hai. Aur ab se... bas yahi ek karan mere liye kaafi hai."

Humare theek peeche khadi Raadhna, jo andhere me hamari baatein sun rahi theen, unki aankhon se aansu beh nikle. Wah dheere se chal kar aage aayin. Unhone Dhriv ke sar par apna haath rakha. 

"Yahi wah pal hai, Dhriv," unka gala ruddh gaya tha, "jiska intezaar tumhare pita ne apni aakhri saans tak kiya tha. Unhe hamesha yah dukh raha ki wah darr ke maare hi jeevan bhar 'Sahi' kaam karte rahe. Kabhi apne dil se, khud ki marzi se Bhalai nahi chun paye. Aaj, tumhare is ek nishchay ne, unki bhatakti atma ko poori tarah swatantra kar diya hai."

Kuch din aur us shanti me bitane ke baad, Dhriv ne wapas apni maa ke paas lautne ka faisla kiya. Vida karte samay Raadhna ne uske mathe ko chooma. "Dhriv, tum jab bhi chaho yahan laut aana. Parantu yaad rakhna, mukti is parvat me nahi hai... mukti tumhare bhitar hai." 

Dhriv ne pairo par jhuk kar unhe pranaam kiya. Pehli baar, bina kisi kadwahat ke, usne unhe apni maa ke saman sammaan diya.

Hum dono aashram se nikal kar wapas gaon ki yatra par chal pade. Pahaadon se utarte waqt Dhriv pichle do dino se bohot shant tha. Achanak, ek pahaadi jharne ke paas aaram karte waqt usne apni jholi se wahi purani bhoj-patra ki parchi nikali aur meri taraf badha di. 

"Chandika," uski aawaz me ek thehrav tha, "us din maine tumhe is patra ka poora sach nahi bataya tha."

Maine hairani se patra khola. Aage ke shabd padhte hi mere haath buri tarah kaanpne lage. Usme likha tha: 
*"Tum us din paida hue the jis din Krupiri ki god sooni ho gayi thi. Uska navjaat balak jivit nahi bacha tha. Krupiri is shok me paagal ho jaati. Tab Raadhna ne apne aur mere, yani apne khud ke navjaat putra ko mujhe de diya, taaki Krupiri ka jeevan bach sake. Dhriv, tum Krupiri ke nahi, Raadhna ke putra ho. Tumhare ragon me us stree ka khoon hai jise tumne ajeevan kosa hai."*

Mere pairo tale zameen khisak gayi. Dhriv apni asli maa ko poori zindagi kostaa raha, aur jis maa ke dukh ne use is shraap ke bhayanak daldal me dhakela, wah uski apni maa thi hi nahi! Yah kaisa khel tha niyati ka?

Dhriv ne pahaadon ki taraf dekhte hue ek thandi saans li. "Socho Chandika, jis Krupiri maa ne mujhe janam bhi nahi diya, unhone mujhe apne khoon se zyada pyaar diya. Aur jis Raadhna ne mujhe janam diya, usne bina kisi adhikaar ya samman ki apeksha ke, apna sabse anmol ansh meri maa Krupiri ko saunp diya. In dono striyon ka balidan itna bada tha ki uske aage mera yah 'shraap' aur mera dukh, mahez ek kankad ke barabar hai."

Ab mujhe samajh me aaya ki aashram chhodte waqt Dhriv ne Raadhna ko 'maa' ke saman samman kyun diya tha. Yah keval shanti nahi thi, yah ek bete ka apni dono maaon ke prati atoot samarpan tha.

Lambi yatra poori karke hum wapas gaon laute. Gaon me pahunchte hi Dhriv seedha apne ghar ki taraf bhaaga. Krupiri mausi ne jab darwaze par Dhriv ko dekha, to unki ankhon me ashcharya, phir chinta uth aayi. Parantu jab unhone uske chehre par ek sachi, sthir shanti dekhi, to unke kande dheele pad gaye. 

Dhriv apni maa ke aangan me rote hue ghutno ke bal gir pada. Usne apne dono hath jod kar maafi maangi—apne poore hriday se, har us galti aur peeda ke liye jo usne unhe di thi. Maa-bete der tak us mitti ke aangan me ek-doosre ko gale lagakar rote rahe. Saalon ka jama hua kachra, aur darawana dard, us din unki aankhon se beh gaya.

Us din ke baad se, Dhriv ne phir kabhi madira ko haath nahi lagaya. Kisi saza ke darr se nahi, aur na hi kisi puraskaar ke lalach me. Balki us gehri samajh se, ki ab us zeher ka uske naye jeevan me koi sthaan nahi tha. 

Shuru me gaon walo ne use sandeh (doubt) ki drishti se dekha. "Dekhte hai yah badlaav kitne din tikta hai," log taane maarte. Parantu jab mahine par mahine beetne lage, aur Dhriv pahaad ki tarah atoot raha, to gaon walo ka sandeh vishwas me badalne laga. Wah phir se gaon ki chaupal me baithne laga, khet me paseena bahane laga, aur gaon ke bhatke hue yuvako ko madira ki lat se bachne ki salah dene laga.

Varshon baad, gaon ke ek samaroh me Dhriv ki mulakat Shivani se hui. Wah ab kisi aur ki patni thi, parantu uski aankhon me Dhriv ke liye abhi bhi ek khamosh sawal tha. Jab Dhriv ne us din rote hue use apna poora sach bataya—apna bhayanak darr, apna shraap, aur us barfeeli raat ki poori sachhai jiske karan usne madira pi thi—to Shivani ki aankhon se aasu beh nikle. Usne Dhriv ko sachche dil se maaf kar diya. Unka prem bhale hi mukammal nahi ho paya tha, parantu us din us adhuri kahani ko ek pavitra shanti mil gayi. Ab unke beech koi gilasikwa nahi tha, keval ek atoot, maun aadar tha. Dhriv ab bhale hi akela tha, parantu uski aatma sadiyon ke bojh se mukt thi.

Mai, Chandraprish, aaj bhi jab gaon ke us chhote-se Peepal ke ped ke paas se guzarta hoon, to mujhe wahi masoom balak yaad aata hai, jo mor pankh lagakar pahaadon ki hawa me sangeet gholta tha. Aur yahi sochkar muskurata hoon, ki asli shraap koi jaadu ya Kripaldev ki madira nahi thi. Asli shraap yah 'soch' hai, ki hum Sahi rasta keval 'Saza' ke darr se chunte hai! 

Jis din insan Sahi rasta isliye chunta hai kyuki wah SAHI hai, bina kisi lalach ya bhay ke... us din har shraap, chahe wah kitna bhi purana aur ziddi kyun na ho, apne aap shishe ki tarah toot kar bikhar jata hai. 

Aur yahi, mere mitro, Dhriv ki is lambi kahani ka sabse bada saar hai.

By Aditya Singh Rajput
